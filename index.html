About

I started editing videos in 2020 with Sheikh Studios, a Dubai-based studio I worked for remotely. There I edited vlogs from raw footage and made thumbnails for YouTube and Facebook.

After that, I worked for some time at Prime News HD, a small local news channel, editing daily news videos.

From July 2023 to May 2024, I worked at Virtue Patch in Lahore. There I edited many different kinds of YouTube videos, funny videos, Snapchat highlights, Cash Cow videos and faceless channel content. Many of these videos reached thousands of views, and this is where I learned how editing changes whether people keep watching a video or leave it.

From June 2024 to January 2025, I worked at InterLink Multi Media in Lahore, making faceless and Cash Cow videos for YouTube channels. My work there also included researching topics before a video was made.

In January 2025, I left InterLink and started freelancing. In January 2026, I set up my own small company, Cutlix Motion, with a team of four video editors. Along with editing, we also do automation work for channels.

My main tool is Adobe Premiere Pro. Colour correction, sound work and thumbnail design are also part of my editing.
